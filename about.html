Our Story

Cake is how I say congratulations

Cake is how I say congratulations. Because a milestone shouldn't just be celebrated; it should be tasted. From a passionate Peterborough kitchen to your center stage, Cakeydoc combines fine artistry with a love for the exact details that make your story unique.

Founder & Baker
Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm the baker behind Cakeydoc

Cakeydoc started the way a lot of good things do — with a cake made for someone I loved, and a room full of people asking who made it. What began as cakes for family celebrations grew, order by order, into a dedicated cake studio serving Peterborough.

I taught myself the craft the way most independent bakers do: by practising relentlessly, studying finishes I admired, and refusing to send out anything I wasn't proud of. Today, every Cakeydoc order — from a single bento cake to a full two-tier centrepiece — still gets that same level of attention.

What hasn't changed is the promise I started with: a cake should look as good as the celebration deserves, and taste even better.

Our Mission

To make every celebration a little sweeter by creating cakes that are designed around the person they're for — not picked off a shelf.

Our Vision

To become Peterborough's most trusted name for custom celebration cakes — known equally for craftsmanship and for genuinely great flavour.

Our Promise

No cake leaves the kitchen unless it meets the standard I'd want for my own family's celebration.

How Far We've Come
