The baker behind it

Welcome to Cakeydoc

Every Cakeydoc cake starts the same way: with your celebration in mind. I'm the baker behind Cakeydoc, based in Peterborough, and I specialise in birthday cakes, children's character cakes, bento cakes and cupcakes for every kind of celebration.

Clean finishes, thoughtful details, and flavour that matches the design — that's the promise behind every box that leaves the kitchen.

The Process

How to place an order

Available sizes: 5"×8" (3 layers), 6"×6" (3 layers), and 6"×8" (4 layers) — plus bento, cupcake and 2-tier options.

01

Choose Your Size

Select 5"×8", 6"×6", 6"×8" — or a bento, cupcake, or 2-tier option from the price guide.

02

Choose Your Design

Pick a design from the gallery or request a custom style — each cake is uniquely crafted.

03

Choose Your Colours

Share your primary and secondary colours so the design fits your celebration perfectly.

04

Choose Your Flavours

Basic: vanilla or chocolate. Premium: coconut, red velvet, or carrot.

05

Share Event Details

Include your event date, collection date, and celebration type so it's ready on time.

06

Celebrant & Toppers

Share the celebrant's age and gender, and let me know if you'd like a name or age topper.
